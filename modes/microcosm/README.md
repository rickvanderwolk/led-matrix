# Microcosm

A tiny world that lives on its own. Hills and hollows, ponds that fill and dry up, grass and trees, rabbits and foxes. Days and nights, seasons and weather. Everything follows from simple, real rules: nothing is scripted and nothing is rescued. Whatever disappears can come back, but only when the conditions allow it. Every world starts differently.

## Settings

In `config.json`, under `"modes": {"microcosm": {...}}`:

- `day_minutes`: length of a day in minutes (default 6)
- `year_days`: days in a year (default 8)
- `start_season`: `spring`, `summer`, `autumn` or `winter` (default random)
- `plants`: `true` to add flowers, fruit and mushrooms (default `false`)

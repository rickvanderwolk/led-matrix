# Terrarium

A small piece of the world seen from the side, like a slice through a snow globe. Sky above, a strip of land in the middle, the ground below. The sky follows the sun through the day: pale blue, golden, a red sunset, the blue hour, a dark night full of slowly turning stars. The moon waxes and wanes and rises later every day. Rain fills the hollow, snow piles up, trees grow and fall. Rabbits graze at dawn and dusk and sleep in their burrows, mice nest deeper down and hoard seeds for the winter, and foxes, owls and buzzards come to hunt them. Birds sow the trees. The land is a window on a bigger world: animals go off to feed out of sight and come back. Everything follows from simple, real rules: nothing is scripted and nothing is rescued.

## Settings

In `config.json`, under `"modes": {"terrarium": {...}}`:

- `day_minutes`: length of a day and night in minutes (default 20)
- `season_minutes`: length of a season in minutes (default 480)
- `start_season`: `spring`, `summer`, `autumn` or `winter` (default random)
